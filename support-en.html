FAQ

  • How do I sign in? Apple or Google only. There is no separate username/password.
  • How do I invite family? In Settings, share the invite code or link.
  • How do I switch families? Settings → Join another family (with the new code). You always belong to one family.
  • Widget not updating? Open Focus once; the widget shows what’s next from your synced agenda.
  • Ads. The Free plan may show ads. Focus PRO removes them.
  • Cancel PRO. iPhone → Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. You can also Restore Purchases from the paywall or Settings.
  • Delete my account. Settings → Delete account. Apple subscriptions are managed separately in system Settings.
  • Microphone issues. Try on a real iPhone; check Microphone and Speech Recognition for Focus in iOS Settings.
