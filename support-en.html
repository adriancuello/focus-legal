FAQ

  • How do I sign in?Apple or Google only. Focus has no separate username/password.
  • How do I invite family?In Settings, share the invite code or link.
  • How do I switch families?Settings → Join another family with the new code. You always belong to one family.
  • Widget not updatingOpen Focus once. The widget shows what’s next from your synced agenda.
  • Ads & Focus PROFree may show ads. PRO removes them. Cancel in iPhone → Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.
  • Delete my accountSettings → Delete account. Apple subscriptions are managed separately in system Settings.
  • Microphone issuesUse a real iPhone close to the mic. Check Microphone and Speech Recognition under iOS Settings → Focus.
