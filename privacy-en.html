Privacy Policy — Focus

Last updated: September 6, 2026 · App: Focus · Bundle ID: aec.focusia

This Privacy Policy describes how the developer of Focus (“we”, “the app”) collects, uses, stores, shares, and deletes personal information when you use Focus on iPhone. By creating an account, joining a family, or using features that send data to our providers, you agree to the processing described here. If you do not agree, do not use the app or delete your account.

Quick summary: Focus stores account and family-organizer data to sync the agenda among members. Text you dictate or type to the assistant may be sent to an AI provider only when you trigger it. The Free plan may show ads (AdMob). We do not sell your data. You can delete data or your account in Settings.

1. Controller

The controller of personal data processed through Focus is the developer listed on the App Store product page (Bundle ID aec.focusia). For privacy requests, use the contact email on that listing or, if available, Contact support in Settings.

2. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • the Focus iOS mobile app;
  • the sync backend associated with the app;
  • authentication, subscriptions, the AI assistant, the document vault, and device permissions.

We do not control third-party sites or services the app may link to (for example, Apple pages to manage subscriptions). Those services have their own policies.

3. Data we process

Depending on how you use Focus, we may process the following categories:

3.1 Account and identity data

  • User identifier generated by the authentication provider.
  • Email or other data Apple or Google share when you sign in (depending on the options you choose; with Apple you may hide your email).
  • Session tokens needed to keep you signed in.

3.2 Family data and content you upload

  • Family name and invite code.
  • Member profiles (name, photo if you add one, color, or other profile fields).
  • Agenda events and tasks (titles, dates, notes, member assignment).
  • Sticky notes.
  • Vault documents (images or other files you upload) and related metadata.
  • Assistant chat history when you use chat.

3.3 Voice assistant and AI data

  • Audio captured by the microphone while you dictate (processed on device for speech recognition per Apple’s system).
  • Transcribed or typed text you send to the assistant.
  • Responses and instructions generated to create or edit agenda items.

Sending data to AI providers happens only when you act (for example, you finish a dictation or send a message), not continuously in the background.

3.4 Subscription and purchase data

  • Free / Focus PRO subscription status.
  • Purchase identifiers and entitlements managed by Apple and, where applicable, RevenueCat (we do not see your full card number).
  • Usage counters tied to plan limits (for example AI uses or documents), needed to enforce Free limits and PRO fair use.

3.5 Technical and device data

  • Basic device and OS information the system or SDKs may need to operate.
  • Local preferences (app language, reminders, calendar sync, onboarding flags).
  • Error or diagnostic logs the system or providers may generate when an operation fails (to the extent enabled).

3.6 Optional data via iOS permissions

  • Microphone / speech recognition: dictation.
  • Camera / photos: scan or save vault documents.
  • Calendars: sync events with iOS Calendar if you enable it.
  • Location when in use: weather on the agenda (if you allow it).
  • Notifications: event reminders if you configure them.

You can deny or revoke these permissions in iOS Settings. Some features will stop working.

4. Purposes

We use data to:

  1. create and authenticate your account;
  2. let you create, join, and manage a shared family;
  3. sync agenda, notes, profiles, and vault across devices and authorized members;
  4. interpret assistant dictations and messages to help organize the agenda;
  5. apply Free limits and Focus PRO fair use;
  6. process and restore purchases via Apple / RevenueCat;
  7. show weather, reminders, or calendar sync when enabled;
  8. show ads on the Free plan (Google AdMob: banner and occasional interstitials); Focus PRO shows no ads;
  9. provide support, prevent abuse, and improve service security;
  10. comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use your family content to personalize ads or to train our own AI models for advertising.

5. Legal bases

Depending on your jurisdiction, processing is based on one or more of: performance of a contract (providing the service you requested), your consent (device permissions, voluntary text sent to AI, certain optional data, and advertising consent forms when applicable), legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, limited operations), and legal obligations where applicable.

6. Who we share data with

We share data only with providers needed to operate Focus, under their own terms:

  • Supabase: authentication, database, and family content sync.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, In-App Purchases, speech / system permissions, App Store.
  • Google: if you choose Continue with Google (Google Sign-In / OAuth); and Google AdMob for Free-plan ads (may use device identifiers and technical advertising data; we request non-personalized ads by default).
  • RevenueCat: subscription status and entitlements.
  • AI providers (e.g. OpenAI or Google Gemini, depending on configuration): text/transcript you send to the assistant, to generate responses and organization instructions.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect rights, safety, and integrity of the service or users, to the extent permitted.

Family members with access will see shared family content (agenda, notes, vault, profiles). You are responsible for who receives the invite code.

7. International transfers

Our providers may process data on servers outside your country (for example, the United States or other regions). By using Focus you understand those transfers may occur subject to each provider’s safeguards.

8. Retention

We retain data while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. If you delete your account, we delete or unlink your user and, depending on backend configuration, family data covered by that deletion flow. Residual copies may remain in backups for a limited period for security and continuity until rotated.

Monthly AI usage counters reset per the calendar cycle implemented in the app. Local preferences may remain on the device until you uninstall the app or clear data.

9. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational security measures (authenticated access, encryption in transit to services, family access controls). No system is 100% secure. Avoid uploading to the vault or dictating to the assistant highly sensitive information (passwords, detailed medical data, full card numbers, etc.) unless strictly necessary.

10. Children

Focus is designed as a family organizer. The App Store / Focus account holder must have legal capacity to accept these terms and, if minors are in the family, is responsible for their use and content about them. We do not target Focus independently at children to collect their data without a responsible adult.

11. Your rights and controls

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, rectify, update, restrict, object to, or request deletion of personal data. In practice, within Focus you can:

  • edit profiles, events, notes, and documents;
  • use Settings → Delete data → Delete all data to erase family content per that feature;
  • use Settings → Delete data → Delete account to request permanent account deletion (when signed in);
  • sign out, revoke system permissions, and manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple Settings → Subscriptions;
  • contact us via the support channel / developer email on the App Store.

Apple purchases and refunds follow Apple’s policies; deleting your Focus account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription.

12. No sale or ads based on your family content

We do not sell your personal information. The Free plan may show third-party ads via AdMob. We do not use your family agenda content (events, notes, documents, chat) to personalize those ads. Focus PRO removes ads.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates the current version. For material changes we will try to notify you in the app or on the App Store listing when reasonable. Continued use after an update means acceptance of the new version, unless law requires other consent.

14. Contact

To exercise rights or ask privacy questions: the developer contact email on the Focus App Store listing, or Settings → Contact support / Report content if shown in your version.
